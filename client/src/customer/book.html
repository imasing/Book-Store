Synopsis

When their estranged father dies, Nell, Agnes and Roo Calloway inherit the Skerry Point light, a debt they cannot pay and forty years of handwritten logbooks. As a winter of storms closes in, the sisters read the entries aloud each night and begin to piece together the one rescue their father never wrote down. Warm, sharp and full of weather, it is a novel about the things families keep burning for each other.
